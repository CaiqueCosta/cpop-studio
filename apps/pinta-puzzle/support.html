FAQ

What devices does Pinta Puzzle support?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Pinta Puzzle is offline-first. You can create and play puzzles without an account or network connection.

Where do my photos go?

Photos you import or capture for puzzles are stored on your device inside the app. They are not uploaded to CPoP Studio or any cloud service operated by us. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Why does the app ask for camera access?

Camera access is optional and only used so you can take a photo to turn into a puzzle. You can decline and still choose photos from your library with the system picker.

How do I delete my puzzles or photos from the app?

Remove individual puzzles from within the app, or delete the Pinta Puzzle app from your device to remove all locally stored puzzle data.

Gameplay tips

Drag pieces to move them; correctly aligned neighbors snap into groups. Pinch to zoom and pan with two fingers. When rotation is enabled, tap a piece to rotate it 90°. Use the eye button for a miniature reference image.
