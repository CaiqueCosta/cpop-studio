FAQ

What is Seabay?

Seabay is a marketplace for boats. Sellers publish listings; buyers browse, save, and message sellers. Seabay connects the two but is not a party to any sale — see the Terms.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse, search, and open listing details without an account. You need an account to save favorites, list a boat, message sellers, report content, or block a user.

How do I report a listing or block someone?

Open a listing and use Report listing or Report user. In a conversation, use the menu to report or block. Blocking stops messages and immediately hides that person’s listings from your feed; we are notified. We review reports and act within 24 hours. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Account settings → Delete account in the app. This removes your profile and cascades to your listings, messages, likes, views, saved searches, and device tokens. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does Seabay use my location?

Only the city you enter. Seabay never requests device location or GPS; it derives approximate coordinates from the city you select so it can show nearby listings.

Why am I not getting notifications?

Make sure you allowed notifications when prompted, or enable them in iOS Settings → Seabay → Notifications. Notifications are optional and used to tell you about new messages.
