FAQ

What is Vessla?

Vessla is a marketplace for boats. Sellers publish listings; buyers browse, save, and message sellers. Vessla connects the two but is not a party to any sale — see the Terms.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Vessla requires an account so you can post listings, save favorites, and message other users.

How do I report a listing or block someone?

Open a listing and use Report to flag content that violates our rules. In a conversation, use Block to stop receiving messages from that user. We review reports and act on violations, typically within 24 hours. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Account settings → Delete account in the app. This removes your profile and cascades to your listings, messages, likes, views, saved searches, and device tokens. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does Vessla use my location?

Only the city you enter. Vessla never requests device location or GPS; it derives approximate coordinates from the city you select so it can show nearby listings.

Why am I not getting notifications?

Make sure you allowed notifications when prompted, or enable them in iOS Settings → Vessla → Notifications. Notifications are optional and used to tell you about new messages.
