Vessla

The boat marketplace for iPhone and iPad

Vessla is a marketplace for boats. Browse listings near you, save the ones you like, and message sellers directly. Sellers can publish a listing with photos and specs in minutes.

Support Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What you can do

  • Discover boats with search and nearby, location-aware suggestions.
  • Save favorites and saved searches to keep track of what you’re after.
  • Sell by publishing a listing with photos, price, and specifications.
  • Message buyers and sellers directly, with push notifications for new messages.

Safety

Listings are moderated before appearing in the marketplace. You can report a listing, and block a user to stop receiving messages from them. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior — see the Terms of Service.
